Origins & Vision

Rooted in volcanic soil. Poured in European silence.

01

The Plant

Raised among coffee vegetation in Guatemala, the true capacity of the seed remained unrevealed until rigorous roasting and extraction sciences were mastered.

02

The European Shift

Relocating for Economics studies, I discovered Europe's reverence for the cup. Treasures like native cardamom (99% exported) found their true ritual here in Belgium.

03

The Danta Ethos

Named after the endangered Central American tapir—the forest's silent gardener—our bar stands as a tribute to the uncelebrated labor of agricultural stewardship.
